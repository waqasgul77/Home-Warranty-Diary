Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 23, 2026

Warranty Diary ("the App") is developed by Waqas MurghiWala. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App handles and how it is used. By using the App, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Data You Enter Into the App

Warranty Diary is an offline, on-device application. Any information you enter — such as product names, purchase dates, warranty periods, receipts details, service records, and reminder settings — is stored only on your device's local storage. This data is never uploaded to, or stored on, any server operated by us. We do not have access to it, and we cannot see, collect, or recover it.

If you use the app's optional Backup / Export feature, a data file is created and saved on your own device, under your control. You decide if and where to share that file — we never receive a copy of it.

2. App Lock (PIN)

If you enable the optional PIN lock feature, your PIN is stored only on your device and is never transmitted anywhere. We cannot see or recover it.

3. Notifications & Reminders

The App can schedule local device notifications to remind you about upcoming warranty expiries or service dates. These reminders are handled entirely by your device's operating system and are not sent through, or visible to, any external server.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App displays ads using Google AdMob. To deliver and measure ads, Google's AdMob SDK may automatically collect certain technical information, which can include:

  • Advertising ID and other device identifiers
  • IP address and approximate location
  • Device type, operating system, and app usage data related to ad interactions

This information is collected and processed by Google in accordance with Google's own privacy policy, not by us directly. Depending on your region and settings, ads may or may not be personalized. You can review or manage your ad personalization preferences in your device's settings, and learn more about how Google handles this data at:

https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

6. Children's Privacy

Warranty Diary is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Data Deletion

Since all of your product and warranty data is stored only on your device, you can delete it at any time by clearing the App's data from your device settings, or by simply uninstalling the App — this permanently removes all locally stored information.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
